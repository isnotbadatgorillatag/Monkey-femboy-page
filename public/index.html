Study Finds Minecraft-Playing Monkey Shows Unexpected Preference in Visual Survey

Researchers say one touchscreen-savvy monkey repeatedly favored images of femboys over images of girls during a highly questionable visual-choice experiment.

By The Daily Primate Science Desk • September 2026

A monkey famous online for playing Minecraft on a touchscreen has become the subject of an equally unusual fictional research study after researchers claimed the animal showed a consistent preference during a visual survey.

According to the imaginary researchers at the Institute for Questionable Animal Research, the monkey, nicknamed Milo, was shown randomized pictures of girls and femboys on a touchscreen. Researchers recorded where Milo looked, which pictures he touched, and whether he returned to an image after being shown another.

“We expected random variation. We did not expect him to develop a type.”

In the fictional experiment, Milo allegedly spent more time looking at the femboy images and repeatedly selected them when given a choice. The researchers emphasized that the result came from a single monkey and could have been affected by completely ordinary factors such as colors, composition, facial expressions, or simple touchscreen curiosity.

After the survey, Milo reportedly returned to Minecraft. In the researchers' joke-filled account, he built a small house with a sign reading “NO GIRLS. Femboys only.” before continuing to play.

Scientists remain extremely unconvinced

The fictional team stressed that the experiment could not establish anything about animal sexuality or broader monkey behavior. A single subject, a tiny sample of images, and an uncontrolled touchscreen environment would make any serious conclusion impossible.

For now, the only certain finding is that Milo apparently has better touchscreen skills than most people expected from a monkey—and possibly stronger opinions about character design.
